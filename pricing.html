FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the FREE plan?

FREE provides the basic CynExtra-AI workspace and the default Premium theme, along with features available to your account.

What does PRO add?

PRO is designed for users who want additional workspace personalization and the PRO theme collection: Neon Blue, Cyber Purple and Ocean Glass.

What is ULTIMATE MODE?

ULTIMATE MODE is the advanced workspace experience. Actual tools, integrations, automation and other advanced capabilities depend on account permissions and available services.

Can I change my theme?

Available themes can be managed from Settings. Theme availability depends on the plan and services connected to your account.
